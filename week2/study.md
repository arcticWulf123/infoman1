A small car repair shop wants to computerize its service records. The shop has many customers, and each customer may bring in one or more cars for repair. Each car has a model, a plate number, and a color, and belongs to exactly one customer. The shop employs several mechanics, each with a name and a specialty (e.g. engine, brakes, electrical). When a car needs work, a mechanic works on it during a scheduled service appointment, and one mechanic may work on many different cars over time — just as one car may be serviced by different mechanics on different visits. Each service appointment has a date and a short repair note.
